Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything you log — foods, goals, water, and your profile — is stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud backend. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I set or change my calorie goal?

Open Settings → Personal Details to update your age, height, weight, and activity level — the app recalculates a suggested daily target. You can also override the target directly from the goal screen.

Can I add my own foods?

Yes. Tap Add Food → Create custom food, enter the nutrition per 100 g, and it becomes searchable like any other item. Manage your custom foods under Settings → Custom Foods.

How do I export my log?

Use Settings → Export CSV to share your complete food log as a spreadsheet file.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

The app follows a dark look by default; switch anytime under Settings → Appearance.

Why do I see ads?

Calorie Counter is free and supported by ads. Ads are non-personalized and appear at the end of content — they never interrupt logging a meal.

I deleted the app — can I recover my log?

Unfortunately no. Because data lives only on your device (never on a server), deleting the app also deletes its data. Export a CSV first if you want a backup.
